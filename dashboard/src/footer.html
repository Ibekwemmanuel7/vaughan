Data: GOES-16 ABI L2 CMIP (NOAA), NOAA-20 ATMS SDR (NOAA CLASS), ERA5 (Copernicus CDS), IMERG (NASA GES DISC), IBTrACS best track. Training archive: 1,057 storm-centred scenes, Atlantic and East Pacific 2017 to 2023. References: Cannon et al. 2024, Deep Learning for Multi-Satellite Precipitation Retrievals (ESS Open Archive, 10.22541/essoar.173430371.11882843); Guerrette et al. 2026, All-sky assimilation impacts of the Tomorrow.io microwave sounder constellation (QJRMS, 10.1002/qj.70106); Song et al. 2023, pseudo-inverse guided diffusion; Chung et al. 2023, diffusion posterior sampling. Code: vaughan, PyTorch. Built with AI-assisted development; the physics decisions are the author's.
