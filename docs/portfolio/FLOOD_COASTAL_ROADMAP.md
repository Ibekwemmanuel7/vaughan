# Flood and coastal impacts roadmap

Status: planned extension of Vaughan. Existing hurricane structure retrieval remains a separate research capability. Portfolio hub: [Lillie US](https://github.com/Ibekwemmanuel7/Lillie_US).

## Proposed first decision

An environmental or asset-management consultant needs to decide which customer sites warrant field inspection after a flood or coastal storm. Confirm buyer access and the decision deadline before choosing a pilot geography or event.

## Work packages

1. Define a bounded event and asset portfolio with dated pre-event conditions and independent reference observations.
2. Ingest suitable radar/optical observations and terrain, tide, gauge or official hazard information as appropriate; record acquisition, availability and processing times.
3. Develop observed flood-extent processing with explicit urban, vegetation, terrain and missing-observation quality flags. Keep modelled and observed extent separate.
4. Add event-related coastal inundation and shoreline-change comparisons, recording tide, image-registration and resolution limitations.
5. Overlay assets and access routes to prioritise inspection. Exposure is not confirmation of damage or interior water depth.
6. Produce reviewed event histories and recovery updates; hand sector decisions to Vantage, Vepris or the customer's adviser.

## Reference and acceptance plan

Freeze event selection, as-of inputs, baselines, reference-label methods, spatial/temporal holdouts and missing-data policy before scoring. Compare with a simple public-event or change-detection baseline on the same eligible cases. Validate false positives, omissions, extent agreement, freshness and customer decision value using independent references and field observations. Include non-flooded controls and difficult urban/vegetated areas. Sample size and pass thresholds must be justified in the pilot specification, not invented after seeing results.

No operational flood-depth, storm-surge, damage, insurance or warning claim is made by this roadmap. These require their own reference measurements, modelling, uncertainty and service validation. Existing hurricane research must not be presented as evidence that this extension works.

## Division with Velo

Vaughan owns event flooding and coastal impacts. Velo owns ongoing water-resource, watershed and water-quality context. They exchange dated geospatial products and provenance through a future Lillie US integration; no circular software dependency is required.
